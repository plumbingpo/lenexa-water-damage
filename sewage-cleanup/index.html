Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. When it happens in Lenexa, Lenexa Restoration is available 24/7 with technicians trained for this work, proper protective gear and disinfectants registered with the EPA, everything needed for a safe decontamination.

The industry classifies sewage as black water, or Category 3, which is the most dangerous and most contaminated type of water damage there is. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. For Category 3 water damage the reference is IICRC S500, and our crews follow it to bring the building back to safe occupancy.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. We also deodorize the affected area with industrial-grade hydroxyl generators and thermal fogging equipment to eliminate lingering sewage odors.

Maintenance Tips for Sewage Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Keep cooking oil, grease and food scraps out of the drain, where they build into clogs
  • Have your sewer line inspected with a camera every 2 to 3 years to catch potential problems
  • Keep downspouts and sump pump discharge out of the sanitary sewer so it does not overload in heavy rain
  • Hair and other debris are easy to stop with simple strainers on tub, shower and sink drains
  • Address slow drains promptly, before they become complete blockages
  • Have roots removed where trees grow by the sewer, before they get inside the pipe and plug it

What a Professional Brings to Sewage Cleanup

Sewage cleanup is not something any homeowner should attempt. Because raw sewage is a biological hazard, the work demands trained people, protective equipment (gloves, boots, respirators and Tyvek suits) and disinfection products registered with the EPA and not sold to the public. Exposure to sewage pathogens can cause serious illness, including gastrointestinal disease, hepatitis, respiratory infections and skin infections.

Professional sewage cleanup also requires proper disposal of contaminated materials as biohazard waste. It is illegal to throw sewage-contaminated insulation, drywall or carpet in the regular trash, and doing so puts the community and the workers who collect it at risk. Certified restoration companies maintain proper waste disposal protocols and relationships with licensed biohazard waste disposal facilities.

When to Call for Sewage Cleanup in Lenexa

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Wet spots on floors or walls accompanied by foul odors
  • Gurgling sounds from toilets or drains when other fixtures are used
  • Sluggish drainage at all fixtures, a sign the main line is blocking up
  • Lower levels or drain openings giving off a heavy sewage odor, sometimes like rotten eggs
  • Wastewater seeping from the sewer cleanout or pooling in the yard above the line
  • Tubs, toilets or floor drains pushing black water back into the room
  • Unexplained illness or gastrointestinal symptoms among household members
